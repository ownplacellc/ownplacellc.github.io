6. AI Studio: Server-Based Writing and Narration

This section applies only if you subscribe to AI Studio and use it.

6.1 No account — an Apple-signed transaction instead

There is still no account. When you subscribe, Apple gives the App a transaction record signed by Apple. The App attaches that signed record to every AI Studio request. Our server verifies Apple's signature and reads from it only: the subscription's original transaction identifier (a number assigned by Apple that stays the same across renewals and devices), the product identifier, the environment (production or sandbox), and the expiry and revocation dates. The signed record itself is not stored on our server; the original transaction identifier is stored with your orders, your permission records, and your hours ledger as the label of "whose subscription paid for this."

The original transaction identifier is a purchase identifier: it contains no name, email, Apple ID, or device identifier, and OWN PLACE LLC has no way to map it to a person. Apple can, and Apple treats it under Apple's own privacy policy. Because it is a persistent, subscription-level identifier, we treat it as personal information (a "User ID" in Apple's App Privacy terminology) and every right in Sections 10–14 applies to it.

The App also sends a shared service token that is the same for every copy of the App. The shared service token is a coarse abuse-control signal and is not user authentication or proof that an unmodified App sent the request. Authorization for subscription data and books depends on successful verification of the Apple-signed transaction. With book orders, the App also sends a random order key that lets the server recognise a repeated request as the same order. The random order key is a pseudonymous order identifier: it does not directly reveal your identity but is linked to the corresponding order while that order exists.

6.2 What our server stores about an order

For each book you order or ask us to narrate, our service keeps one record containing: your request text (for written books) or the text you supplied (for narration), requested length, language, generated title and chapter text, narration file names and durations, technical state and timestamps, the original transaction identifier, the quota window it was charged to, and the seconds of audio charged. Records live in a Modal Labs data store; audio files live on a Modal Labs storage volume. For each writing-mode order, the record also contains the technical request identifiers returned by OpenRouter.

The order text, the generated chapters, the title, and the audio are held as temporary working storage only for the time needed to run and deliver the order: encrypted, subject to an application-level time-to-live, and excluded from OWN PLACE LLC's application logs and crash reports. We do not create separate content backups. Processor backup copies, if any, are handled as described below. They are deleted as described in Section 6.4 ("no post-delivery server retention").

Separately, for each subscription we keep the permission records described in Section 6.6, the hours ledger described in Section 6.4, and, if you have reported content or we have had to act on abuse, a note of that action (book identifier, date, reason category, and the resulting restriction, if any).

6.4 Retention and deletion on the server — no post-delivery server retention

We call this model "no post-delivery server retention."

  • Temporary working storage. The order text, the generated chapters, the title, and the audio are stored on Modal as temporary working storage only for the time needed to run the order and deliver it: encrypted, subject to an application-level time-to-live, and excluded from OWN PLACE LLC's application logs and crash reports. We do not create separate content backups. Processor backup copies, if any, are handled as described below.
  • What counts as delivery. "Delivery is confirmed only after the App has durably saved every chapter on the device." A successful network download is not by itself delivery.
  • Deletion after delivery. Immediately after delivery of the final chapter is confirmed, OWN PLACE LLC deletes its server copy of the order — text and audio alike. OWN PLACE LLC cannot recover or re-deliver the book from its server after deletion; the App may still restore the user's own saved iCloud copy. This is disclosed to you before your first order and on the paywall.
  • If delivery is never confirmed. "If full delivery is not confirmed within seven days after the Service accepts the order, we treat the order as failed, stop further processing, delete all server-side order content, and automatically restore all hours reserved or charged for that order. Chapters already saved on your device remain available."
  • Deleting sooner. Content is deleted sooner if you delete the book in the App (the App sends a delete request to the service; if the phone is offline the request is retried later) or when you ask us (Section 10).
  • Exceptions. "We do not retain order content after confirmed delivery, except content you separately submit in a report or support request and content that law requires us to preserve." This covers content reports and copyright disputes, reports required under 18 U.S.C. § 2258A, and lawful preservation demands.
  • Your own copy. "After delivery, Talefall saves the book on this device and, if iCloud is available and the save succeeds, in your private iCloud storage. OWN PLACE LLC deletes its server copy after the final chapter has been saved on this device and cannot restore it. If iCloud is unavailable or full, export your own backup before deleting the App." The App stores the copy through Apple's iCloud service under your Apple account. OWN PLACE LLC does not access or control that copy. Access to your saved copy does not depend on an active subscription, and it is not "cloud storage" offered as a subscription benefit. You can also export a book manually with Export to Files. A privacy deletion request addressed to us does not delete your own iCloud copy; the delete-book button in the App offers to remove the local and the iCloud copy.
  • Deletion while an order is running. If you request deletion while an order is active, we stop further processing as soon as technically possible, delete OWN PLACE LLC's server-side working content, and restore the affected hours under Terms Section 4.2. We also notify OpenRouter where it may still hold a limited copy. After delivery we confirm to you that the server copy has already been deleted; the hours ledger is handled separately below.
  • Hours ledger (per original transaction identifier: seconds of audio charged per order, quota window, purchased extra hours by Apple transaction identifier, restored hours) and subscription-notification records received from Apple (event type, expiry, revocation) are retained for seven years after the last related financial event, then deleted or irreversibly aggregated. They contain no name and no book content.
  • Permission records (Section 6.6) — containing only the document or permission version, the time, and the original transaction identifier — are retained with the hours ledger for the same period, because they document the basis on which paid work was done.
  • Deletion-request records. When we fulfil a deletion request we keep a minimal record of the request itself (date, a keyed hash of the original transaction identifier — not the identifier itself — and what was deleted; no content) for two years as evidence that we complied.
  • Content reports and enforcement notes are kept for up to 24 months after resolution, longer only for legal claims or where the law requires preservation.
  • Read-aloud passages (Section 5, "read a passage aloud") are not stored.
  • Backups. We do not create separate content backups, and Modal's storage is not separately archived by us. Where a processor keeps backup copies, they expire on that processor's next scheduled expiration cycle after deletion and, for Washington consumer health data, no later than six months after an authenticated request. Modal request logs expire after one day.

6.5 Content limits and safety controls

The language model has no access to your library, your other books, or anything on your device. It sees only the request you typed and what it generated for that order.

8. Usage Counters (optional, off unless you turn them on)

When you allow it, Talefall sends a small set of counters, carrying no identifier of you or your device, describing the steps of a session: that the App was opened, that the library was shown, that the demo book was opened, that playback started, that thirty seconds were played, that the add button was tapped, that an import succeeded or failed, that the trial banner or the purchase screen was shown, and that the App was closed. Each counter carries only its name and the number of seconds since the App was opened; a failed import also carries the file type (for example m4b), never the file name or its location. The batch carries the App version and your device language, and a random number that exists only to group one session's counters together and is not stored by the service.

These counters never contain what you listen to or read: no book titles, no text, no file names, no paths, no bookmarks or notes. We do not attach a persistent user, subscription, or device identifier to counters and do not attempt to link them to a person; there is no advertising identifier and no account, and Talefall uses no third-party analytics SDK. Our server accepts only the fields listed above and discards anything else without looking at it. Ordinary server logs may temporarily contain an IP address and request timestamp for up to one day (Section 9); analytics storage is segregated from those logs, and we do not join the two. Because of that one-day log we describe the counters as identifier-free rather than absolutely anonymous. The counters are not used to track you across apps or sites, are not shared with anyone, and are deleted after 90 days. We ask once, the default answer is no, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy. The counters are not offered to, and are not sent from, devices located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (Section 14).
